I am pursuing a master's degree in Artificial Intelligence at **Persian Gulf University**, after completing my bachelor's degree in Computer Science at **Vali-e-Asr University of Rafsanjan**. My interests center on artificial intelligence, computer systems, software engineering, algorithms, and the infrastructure that keeps useful software reliable.

At the university, I have served as a teaching assistant for courses spanning computer systems, data structures and algorithms, advanced programming, and introductory programming. I enjoy making difficult technical ideas approachable and helping students turn theory into working programs.
